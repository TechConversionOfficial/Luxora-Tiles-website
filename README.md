## About Luxora Tiles

[Luxora Tiles](https://luxoratiles.com/) is an Indian decorative surfaces brand specialising in premium metallic tiles and sanitaryware. Operating from Morbi, Gujarat, the company develops products intended to bring distinctive finishes, visual depth and a luxury character to residential and commercial interiors.

Its product range focuses on decorative tiles in **gold, silver and rose-gold finishes**, alongside sanitaryware designed to complement contemporary bathrooms and interior spaces.

According to the company's published history, its origins trace back to **2011**, when Himalaya Decorators began manufacturing decorative metallic tiles. The business describes subsequent expansion through Amrut Decorators in 2014, an introduction of in-house golden sanitaryware in 2020 and further developments in metallic finishing techniques.

Today, the Luxora brand brings its product expertise together within a single identity focused on decorative surfaces, modern design and material finishes.

The website serves several audiences:

- **Architects and interior designers** exploring decorative materials, metallic surfaces and finish combinations for client projects.
- **Homeowners** researching statement walls, bathrooms, kitchens and other residential applications.
- **Builders and developers** evaluating tile formats and product suitability for residential or commercial spaces.
- **Dealers and distributors** seeking product information, catalogue access and business enquiries.
- **Commercial buyers** comparing distinctive surface options for hospitality, retail and other interior environments.

For a business where texture, finish and appearance strongly influence buying decisions, an effective website must do more than list products. It needs to help visitors understand the material range, visualise applications and identify the next step towards sourcing or purchasing.

## The Website Challenge

Decorative tiles are a design-led product category. Customers rarely make decisions based on specifications alone.

The appearance of a tile changes with lighting, room size, surface texture and surrounding materials. Metallic finishes introduce an additional consideration because reflective properties can look different depending on the environment.

For Luxora Tiles, the digital challenge is to communicate these differences while keeping the product range accessible to both industry professionals and homeowners.

A potential customer might arrive with several questions:

- Which metallic finishes are available?
- What tile sizes can be considered for a particular project?
- How do matt, glossy and other finishes differ?
- How many tiles will be required for a room?
- Where can the products be sourced?
- How can an architect, dealer or property owner contact the business?

These questions influence the website's information structure.

The goal is to make the brand's product collections understandable while giving visitors practical tools to move from initial interest towards an informed enquiry.

The public portfolio does not include an independently documented audit of an earlier Luxora website, so these are the business and user-experience challenges addressed by the visible digital experience rather than verified historical defects.

## How the Website Addresses the Brief

The Luxora Tiles website combines visual presentation with product information and practical selection tools.

### 1. Establishing a Premium Brand Identity

The homepage introduces Luxora as a luxury decorative tiles and sanitaryware brand.

Its messaging places attention on craftsmanship, finish quality and the visual impact of premium surfaces.

Rather than presenting the company solely as a tile manufacturer, the website positions its products within the wider context of contemporary interiors.

This provides a clear starting point for architects, designers, developers and homeowners discovering the brand.

### 2. Organising Product Discovery Around Sizes and Finishes

Tile selection is influenced by more than appearance.

Dimensions, finish type and design suitability all affect how a product can be used.

The website presents multiple available tile sizes, including:

- 100 × 100 mm
- 300 × 300 mm
- 300 × 450 mm
- 300 × 600 mm
- 600 × 600 mm
- 600 × 1200 mm

Additional formats are also listed on the website.

Displaying size information helps visitors understand the breadth of the range before making a detailed product enquiry.

*Product collection presentation featuring decorative wall tiles, available dimensions and premium surface designs.*

### 3. Highlighting Metallic Colour Collections

A defining characteristic of the Luxora product range is its emphasis on metallic surfaces.

The website identifies three primary colour families:

- **Gold:** a decorative metallic finish suited to statement surfaces and visually rich interiors.
- **Silver:** a cooler metallic option for modern architectural and interior applications.
- **Rose Gold:** a warmer metallic appearance for contemporary decorative combinations.

This colour-led structure makes it easier for visitors to understand Luxora's distinctive product positioning.

### 4. Explaining Surface Finish Options

The website also introduces the way finishes influence the appearance of a tile.

Published finish categories include:

- **Matt:** a softer, low-reflection appearance.
- **Glossy:** a highly reflective finish.
- **Matt-Granulla:** a textured, granular matt presentation.
- **Matt-Luster:** a combination of a matt surface and subtle lustre.

These descriptions help explain the visual differences buyers may need to consider before requesting samples or visiting a dealer.

Actual product suitability, durability and technical performance must be confirmed against the relevant manufacturer's specifications.

### 5. Integrating a Practical Tile Calculator

One of the website's useful features is the **Luxora Tiles Calculator**.

Estimating tile quantities can be confusing for customers unfamiliar with flooring or wall-coverage calculations.

The calculator provides inputs for:

- Room length in feet
- Room width in feet
- Tile size in millimetres

It then presents estimated coverage and quantity information, including:

- Total area in square metres
- Total area in square feet
- Estimated required tiles
- Estimated required boxes

The published interface also advises allowing additional material for cutting and wastage.

This feature connects product browsing with practical project planning.

The calculator's presence is verifiable on the website; this README does not claim its estimates have passed an independent mathematical or engineering audit.

### 6. Supporting Catalogue-Based Enquiries

The website provides a route to its product catalogue, allowing customers and industry professionals to explore available collections.

Catalogue access is particularly relevant to a decorative-surface brand because customers often need to compare several designs, dimensions and finishes before making an enquiry.

It also supports conversations between the brand, architects, project consultants and dealers.

### 7. Connecting Customers With Dealers

The **Where To Buy** section provides an important bridge between online product exploration and real-world sourcing.

Visitors can move from researching a collection to finding information about authorised dealers and availability.

This supports a business model where product discovery may happen online while final selection, sampling and purchasing are arranged through physical distribution channels.

### 8. Providing Brand Background and Enquiry Routes

The About section presents the company's published development timeline and its focus on decorative metallic surfaces.

The website also includes a Contact section with business details, helping visitors reach the company for product enquiries, sourcing requirements and related questions.

Together, these elements help make the website useful to visitors at different stages of their decision journey.

## Project Visuals and Design Direction

Luxora Tiles belongs to a category where the visual presentation of the product is central to the buying decision.

The website's published content places emphasis on metallic colours, decorative patterns, surface finishes and their possible use in interior spaces.

The portfolio project can be understood through four core presentation areas.

### Premium Brand and Homepage Presentation

The homepage introduces the Luxora brand and its positioning in decorative tiles and sanitaryware.

Its content connects premium surfaces with contemporary residential and commercial interiors.

The objective is to establish what Luxora specialises in and help visitors move towards its products, catalogue and enquiry options.

### Tile Collections and Finish Presentation

The product content brings together tile sizes, colours and finish types.

For architects and designers, these details help distinguish available surface options before seeking additional specifications or physical samples.

The finish section also gives the brand an opportunity to explain how lighting and texture affect appearance.

### Interactive Tile Calculator

The calculator provides an additional utility beyond ordinary product browsing.

Instead of requiring a visitor to estimate quantities manually, the interface provides a starting point for material planning based on room dimensions and tile size.

For practical purchasing decisions, these estimates should be checked against the project's installation requirements.

### Dealer and Enquiry Experience

The Where To Buy and Contact sections provide routes for visitors who are ready to move beyond initial research.

This is especially important for products where physical availability, samples, supply arrangements and professional advice can influence the final decision.

### Responsive Website Presentation

A website serving both homeowners and industry professionals should make its collections, specifications and contact information usable on smaller screens.

Mobile visitors may need to browse tile finishes, check product information or contact the business while visiting a project site or showroom.

The responsive experience should therefore maintain readable product details, accessible navigation and practical enquiry controls across different screen sizes.

## Quality, Accessibility and Website Standards

The following standards are relevant when reviewing a premium manufacturing and product-discovery website. They are evaluation references, **not claims that Luxora Tiles has passed formal certification or independent technical audits**.

- **[Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals):** Evaluate loading speed, responsiveness and visual stability, particularly on pages containing large product photography and interactive elements.

- **[WCAG 2.2 Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/):** Review accessible navigation, readable text, colour contrast, form labels and keyboard usability, including the tile calculator.

- **[Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials):** Assess crawlability, useful product information, search-friendly page structure and relevant content for decorative tiles and sanitaryware.

- **[Schema.org](https://schema.org/):** Consider accurate organisation, product, breadcrumb and relevant business structured data where the website supports it.

- **Responsive Web Design:** Ensure catalogue content, product dimensions, contact routes and the calculator remain usable across desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

- **Product Information Accuracy:** Verify dimensions, finishes, technical properties and catalogue availability against the manufacturer's current published specifications.

- **Privacy and Enquiry Handling:** Review applicable privacy requirements for contact forms, dealer enquiries and other customer information collected through the website.

No independent performance report, accessibility audit, structured-data validation or security assessment has been supplied for this project record.
